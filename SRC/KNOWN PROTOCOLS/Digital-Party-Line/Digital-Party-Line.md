# Digital Networked Party-Line

**Family:** Digital Networked Party-Line
**Layer:** Multi-channel audio plus channel selection, call signalling and configuration.

## What it is
Party-line functionality carried digitally over structured cable or IP. Many channels on one cable, with power and configuration delivered to the beltpacks.

## How to connect to a device
- Beltpacks daisy-chain or home-run on shielded network cable from a main station that supplies power
- Main stations can link to each other over IP
- Configured from the main station's front panel or web page

## How to talk to a panel
- Beltpack users pick their channels on the pack
- A matrix panel reaches a party-line channel through the main station's 4-wire or IP interface, where the channel appears as one port

## What it carries
Multi-channel digital audio and control.

## Trunking notes
Enters the trunking system through the main station interface, one port per channel.
