# Wireless Beltpack System

**Family:** Wireless Beltpack System
**Layer:** Audio plus key and call control for wireless users.

## What it is
Wireless intercom using licence-free digital radio bands. Base stations or antennas connect to the wired system; beltpacks roam between them.

## How to connect to a device
- Antennas or transceivers connect to the wired system over Ethernet with PoE, or over dedicated cable
- Beltpacks pair or register to the system by touch, cable or over-the-air
- Antenna placement and channel planning set coverage and capacity

## How to talk to a panel
- Each beltpack is a normal port in the system with keys assigned in the configuration tool
- Roaming between antennas is handled by the system
- Standalone base stations often expose 4-wire or standard IP audio for connection to another brand

## What it carries
Digital audio and control over radio.

## Trunking notes
Wireless users trunk like any other port once on the wired side.
